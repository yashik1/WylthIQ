# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@wylthiq.com** with what you found and how to reproduce it.
Please do not open a public issue for anything exploitable.

You should get an acknowledgement within 3 working days. This is a small
project, so please allow a reasonable window to fix something before
disclosing it publicly.

## What this app holds

Worth knowing when judging the impact of a finding:

- **Accounts** — email address, bcrypt password hash, optional username.
- **Saved companies, trade journal entries and saved screens**, each owned by
  one account and scoped by `user_id` in the query itself.
- **Newsletter subscribers** — email address only, double opt-in.
- **No payment details.** Billing is paused, and card data never touches this
  application even when it is switched on; Stripe holds it.
- **No market data of our own.** Prices come from third-party providers under
  personal-use licences and are not resold.

## In scope

- Authentication and session handling
- Access to another account's data (IDOR)
- Injection of any kind
- Unauthenticated access to expensive or scheduled endpoints
- Leakage of secrets, tokens or infrastructure detail

## Out of scope

- Missing headers with no demonstrated impact
- Rate limits on endpoints that cost nothing to serve
- Reports generated by a scanner with no working proof
- Social engineering, physical access, or denial of service by volume

## Notes for reviewers

The application deliberately answers identically whether or not an email
address is registered, on both the password-reset and newsletter forms. That
is anti-enumeration, not a broken response.

Scheduled endpoints under `/api/cron/*` refuse to run at all unless
`CRON_SECRET` is configured, and `/api/health` returns only liveness to an
anonymous caller.
